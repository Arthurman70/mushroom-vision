How the review is recorded

Each annotation stores the original image hash, source partition, AI reviewer identity, observation size, visibility, evidence, and revision. Contact-sheet judgments remain incomplete. Trees, leaves, hats, and stumps are context until an explicitly inspected nonfungal region is boxed; context tags never become negative training labels.

Original-image observations take precedence over previews. A later uncertain original review retires an older complete judgment. Previous revisions remain available below each image.
