“Extra boxes” are all remaining detections after at most one box is assigned to the selected positive reference. They can include duplicate overlaps or objects elsewhere. Incomplete references cannot establish that every extra box is a false alarm. Control alerts come from a separate negative-reference set, whose authority is stated above. A selected region can span a cluster, so detecting one cap may still miss the region-overlap rule. This measures selected-region localization, not species identification or population accuracy.

PIXELS CAN ILLUSTRATE DISTANCE

A camera assumption,
not a flight measurement.

A 5 cm target and a focal length of 1,380 pixels give these ideal geometric widths: target pixels = 1,380 × 0.05 ÷ metres

10 m6.9 px wide
20 m3.45 px wide
50 m1.38 px wide
100 m0.69 px wide

These distances are illustrative, not measured detection rates. Your camera calibration, target size and angle, blur, lighting and occlusion change the image. A larger cluster spans more pixels at the same distance. Synthetic shrinking does not reproduce all those effects.
